## Completion rules

A week is complete when you can explain its concept, produce its artifact and pass its checkpoint. Mark “in progress” when a tutorial has been followed but you cannot explain it yet. Repeat failed cases; do not advance just because a calendar week ended.

Keep a simple record of **date → action → expected result → actual result → evidence → next step**. All new filename suggestions in lessons describe files you will create as you study.

## Milestones

- **After Week 3:** explain a browser/server exchange and read small functions.
- **After Week 7:** describe common vulnerabilities and match them to specific defenses.
- **After Week 11:** demonstrate the local notes app's ownership checks and regression tests.
- **After Week 16:** explain a simulated transfer/payment design and its remaining limitations.

No paid tools, cloud hosting or live payment accounts are required. A browser and text editor are enough to start. Python and Git arrive when needed; Django arrives in Week 8.
